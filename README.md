# Stationery Store Management System

A full-stack stationery e-commerce and management system built with PHP and MySQL.

📌 Overview

This project is a web-based stationery store that allows customers to browse and purchase stationery products online, 
while administrators can manage products, categories, users (staff management) , suppliers, and customer orders.

The system is organized around three education levels:

- Primary School
- High School
- University

✨ Features

Customer's Side

- User registration and login
- Browse products
- Browse products by education level
- Browse products by category
- View product details
- Add products to cart
- Increase and decrease cart quantities
- Remove products from cart
- Checkout
- Choose delivery or pickup
- Enter delivery information
- Cash on Delivery
- Order confirmation

### Admin's Side

- Admin authentication
- Dashboard
- Education level management
- Category management
- Product management
- Inventory management
- Stock In
- Stock Out
- Supplier management
- Order management
- Order item management
- User management
- Staff permissions
- Delivery information management
